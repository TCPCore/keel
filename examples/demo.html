The replay reproduces the mechanism, not the magnitude. At 1x nothing happens; at 2x and 4x it sits at the method's ceiling. The derivative at 0.188 is between the regimes, and the per-module intensity is the parameter this extraction does not recover. The completeness claim is withdrawn for that reason.

Method notes

Scorer calibration: deterministic refusal classifier agreed with the judge on 53 percent of 96 responses, then 78 percent after folding typographic quotes and matching the refusal verb. The remaining disagreements favour the judge, so the judge is primary. The replay tolerance and decision rule were pre-registered in docs/REPLAY_PROTOCOL.md before the 32-prompt run.
