and review at speed, the owner decides and signs. This file records who authored
what, so a reviewer can trace any claim or diff to its source.

## How attribution is recorded

- **`Co-authored-by: Muse Code`** on any commit that contains generated code.
- **`Audited-by:`** trailer on every commit; the pre-push hook refuses commits
  without one.
- **`audit/<task>.md`** records the brief, the Muse session id, what was checked,
  and every correction applied to the generated work.
- **Muse session ids** are in the audit records, so a generated diff can be traced
  to a transcript under `.tools/runs/`.

Attribution is checked, not asserted: the trailers are enforceable, and the audit
records name the specific defects that were corrected.

## Authorship and licence

Agents cannot hold copyright or accept a licence. **cptlight is the author of
record** and the person who accepts the model licences, the event terms, and the
Apache-2.0 grant in `LICENSE`. Agents are credited for the work they did; they are
not rights holders, and `CITATION.cff` names the project rather than listing them as
authors.

## Updating this file

Add a row to "Authored by Muse" when a generated task lands, and add a participant
when a person joins. Do not credit an agent without a session id or a commit range;
"an AI helped" is not provenance. If a contribution is corrected, record the
correction rather than rewriting the credit.
